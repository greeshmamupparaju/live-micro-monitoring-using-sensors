![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/553e44a8-bdea-405f-90de-598dec2af31c)Project Summary: 
Monitoring microbial activity in ponds is crucial for maintaining water quality and supporting aquatic ecosystems.
This project uses smart sensors and AI to automate real-time monitoring of key water parameters like pH, turbidity, temperature, and Total Dissolved Solids (TDS). 
Machine learning models analyze collected data to detect microbial activity and predict potential contamination.
Alerts are generated when water quality deviates from safe levels, enabling quick corrective action.
The goal is to maintain ecological balance, improve water quality management, and enhance environmental sustainability.
